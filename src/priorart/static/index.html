Similar Patent Finder

Describe your invention in natural language. The tool finds the closest patents in the loaded corpus, even when they use other terms, and shows the exact passage that makes each one close, so you can check it yourself.
Demo project. This tool gives a first look, this is not a comprehensive search. This is not legal advice and does not say whether anything is new or patentable.
Known limits : general purpose AI models and small subsets of patents corpus are used
